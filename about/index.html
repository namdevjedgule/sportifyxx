Our Story

Built On the Field, Not In a Boardroom

SportifyX began in 2011 with a single football batch of eleven kids on a borrowed ground on the outskirts of Pune. There was no academy name yet - just a coach, a whistle, and a conviction that structured, honest coaching shouldn't be reserved for metro cities.

Word spread. Parents asked for cricket. Then badminton. Then chess for the kids who preferred a board to a pitch, and karate and lathi kathi for families who wanted discipline and tradition alongside sport. Fifteen years on, that one batch has grown into a full academy running seven disciplines across Pune - still coached with the same standard we started with.

2011
First football batch begins with 11 students on a borrowed ground.
2014
Cricket and Badminton programs launch; first dedicated training ground leased.
2018
Chess, Karate, and Lathi Kathi added - SportifyX becomes a full multi-sport academy.
2021
First state-level selections; SportifyX begins hosting inter-school tournaments.
2026
7 disciplines, 2,200+ students coached, and counting.
What Drives Us

Vision & Mission

Two simple ideas guide every program we run, from a first trial session to a state-level trial.

Our Vision

To make structured, high-quality sports coaching a standard part of growing up in every Tier I and Tier II city in India - not a privilege reserved for a few.

Our Mission

To build a certified coaching pathway across seven disciplines that takes a student from their very first session to competitive readiness, while keeping sport enjoyable at every step.
