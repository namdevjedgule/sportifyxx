About SportifyX

Built On the Field, Not In a Boardroom

SportifyX started as a single football batch on a borrowed ground in Pune. Today we run structured coaching across seven disciplines - Cricket, Football, Badminton, Lawn Tennis, Chess, Karate, and Lathi Kathi - for students who are just starting out and ones chasing state selection.

Every program follows the same standard: certified coaches, a real curriculum, and a pathway that doesn't stop at the first trophy.
