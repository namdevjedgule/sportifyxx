Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations, replacements, and refunds work for SportifyX coaching programs and events.

Home/Refund & Cancellation Policy

Order Cancellations

Order cancellations may be accepted before processing/shipping, subject to our discretion. Certain products/services may not be eligible for cancellation once the order has been confirmed. Any request for cancellation must be raised within 12 hours of placing the order.

Replacements

Purchases made from us cannot be returned after the delivery of goods or the performance of services is complete. If the goods you ordered were damaged in transit or were defective, you can request a replacement order within 15 days of receiving the goods. We will process the replacement orders after we validate the damage and perform required checks.

Refunds

Any request for a refund must be submitted within 8 days of delivery and is applicable only for:

  • Event/Coaching cancellation by the organizers
  • Duplicate payments or technical errors during transaction

You must notify us within 2 days of the transaction or event. Refunds will be processed after we validate the request and perform quality checks.

Approved refunds will be credited back to the customer's original mode of payment within 5–7 working days.
