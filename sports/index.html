Cricket

Batting technique, bowling mechanics, and match-reading, coached with nets and match-simulation drills.

U8–U19Age Range
6Batches/Week
Turf + NetsFacility

Students move from tape-ball fundamentals into hardball training as they progress into the Performance track, with select students trialed for state-level age-group tournaments.

  • Turf ground + indoor net facility

Discipline 02

Football

Ball mastery, positional understanding, and match fitness for every age group.

U6–U19Age Range
7Batches/Week
11v11 / 7v7Formats

Football training starts with movement literacy and ball control before moving into positional play and small-sided games that build decision-making under pressure. Younger batches play 7-a-side; older batches train and compete in full 11-a-side formats.

Match-intelligence sessions — reading space, pressing triggers, transition play — are introduced from the Development stage onward, alongside structured fitness and injury-prevention work.

  • Full-size turf + 7-a-side training pitch
  • Position-specific technical drills
  • Inter-academy friendly matches monthly

Discipline 03

Badminton

Footwork, racket control, and rally intelligence from the very first rally.

U8–U19Age Range
5Batches/Week
Indoor CourtsFacility

Badminton coaching begins with grip, footwork patterns, and basic strokes, before layering in deception, rally construction, and doubles-specific tactics as students progress. All sessions run on indoor wooden courts to keep conditions match-standard year-round.

Students are grouped by skill level within each age band, so beginners and competitive players train at a pace suited to them.

  • 4 indoor wooden courts
  • Singles & doubles tactical training
  • Monthly in-house rating tournaments

Discipline 04

Lawn Tennis

Groundstrokes, serve mechanics, and court coverage on real lawn-tennis courts.

U8–U19Age Range
5Batches/Week
Clay CourtsFacility

Lawn tennis coaching covers the four core strokes — forehand, backhand, serve, and volley — with progressive drills that build consistency before power. Older students move into point-play and match strategy, including doubles positioning.

Sessions run on clay courts, which reward technique over raw power and help build a durable, injury-conscious playing style from a young age.

  • 2 clay courts on-site
  • Individual stroke-correction video review
  • Quarterly in-house ladder tournament

Chess

Openings, tactics, and endgame technique, from first moves to tournament rating.

U8–OpenAge Range
3Batches/Week
FIDE-RatedCoaching

Our chess program is led by a FIDE-rated coach and covers the full arc of the game — opening principles, middlegame tactics, and endgame technique — through a mix of board work, puzzle sets, and annotated game review.

Students who want to compete are prepared for FIDE-rated tournaments, with dedicated openings prep built around each student's own repertoire.

  • FIDE-rated head coach
  • Personalized opening repertoire building
  • Rated tournament prep & annotated review

Discipline 06

Karate

Kata, kumite, and belt-graded discipline in a structured martial-arts curriculum.

U6–U19Age Range
4Batches/Week
3rd DanSensei Rank

Karate training follows a traditional belt-graded curriculum covering kata (forms), kumite (sparring), and self-defense fundamentals, alongside the discipline and etiquette that come with martial arts practice. Belt gradings are held twice a year.

Classes are grouped by belt level rather than strictly by age, so students of similar skill train and grade together.

  • 3rd Dan black-belt sensei
  • Bi-annual belt grading examinations
  • Self-defense & discipline-focused curriculum

Discipline 07

Lathi Kathi

Traditional stick-fighting technique, footwork, and defensive form, rooted in Maharashtra's own martial tradition.

U10–AdultAge Range
3Batches/Week
15 yrsGuru Experience

Lathi Kathi is one of Maharashtra's own traditional martial arts, built around stick-based offense and defense, footwork, and timing. Our program is one of the few structured coaching options for this discipline in the region, taught by a guru with 15 years of practice.

Sessions combine traditional forms with modern conditioning, and students progress through a structured skill ladder rather than an informal hobby-class pace.

  • Traditional Maharashtrian martial art
  • Guru-led, 15 years of practice
  • Structured skill ladder, not informal classes
