SportifyX Kharadi is a Club 360 facility built for both casual play and serious training - a full-size football turf for 5-a-side and 7-a-side games, plus four indoor wooden badminton courts that run year-round regardless of weather.

Slots are available by the hour, seven days a week. Whether it's a Sunday match with friends or a weekday badminton session, you can book directly or reach out on WhatsApp to check live availability.

  • Full-size outdoor football turf
  • 4 indoor wooden badminton courts
  • Open 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily
